# Edge cases — academic management (Proesc)

Harvest: 2026-08-14.

## Trilha blocked without payment plan

Trail cannot send until **plano de pagamento** defined — secretariat may confirm matrícula
but guardian sees nothing; common year-start failure mode.
([trilha](https://suporte.proesc.com/hc/pt-br/articles/17165116960279))

## Rematrícula without next exercício

Rematrícula trail requires next school year created first — ordering mistake blocks entire cohort.

## Multi-signer contracts

Optional multiple guardians must sign; partial completion leaves matrícula in limbo.

## Grades not visible in portal

Dedicated troubleshooting article — causes include unpublished boletim, hidden disciplina,
permission mismatch; families open tickets via Lia.
([notas não aparecem](https://suporte.proesc.com/hc/pt-br))

## Individual lessons in diary

Separate lengthy article for individual lessons — implies default diary flow is class-wide;
edge pedagogy needs extra steps (15+ in extraction heuristic).

## Class transfer vs finance

Reclassificar turma may leave débitos on old turma/plan — finance articles cross-link.

## Boletim hide flags

Hiding disciplina or final grade can confuse guardians who expect full transcript; coordination
must coordinate with pedagogy before publish.

## Diary delivery compliance

Analytics exposes teachers who did not “entregar” diário — labor/process pressure at bimestre close.

## Certificate module limitations

Certificate environment does not design layout — requires external 1062×732 template; support
explicitly excludes layout help.

## Public school segment

Escola pública category (4 articles) may include rules not applicable to private schools — filter
when anchoring PRDs.

## AEE / inclusion modules

Módulos extras document AEE reporting — specialized regulatory domain adjacent to mainstream diary.
