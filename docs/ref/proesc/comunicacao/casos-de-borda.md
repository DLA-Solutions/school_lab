# Edge cases — communication (Proesc)

Harvest: 2026-08-14.

## Limited push notification types

Only rotina, recados, and finance push — **comunicados do not push** per overview (inferred
from explicit list); guardians may miss broadcasts without habit of opening app.

## Module not enabled

Overview article routes schools to sales/support to enable Agenda — families see nothing until
commercial step completes.

## Comunicado comments moderation

When likes/comments enabled, schools lack public guidance on moderation/LGPD for child photos
in threads (gap in corpus).

## Lia vs family support

Families directed to school secretariat; Lia is staff-facing — reduces vendor load but school
becomes tier-1 for app issues.

## Email cadastro update blocked

Troubleshooting article for email update failures — blocks recados and login recovery.

## Teacher not on disciplina

Cannot add professor to disciplina — affects diary/comms indirectly; appears in problem articles.

## Multi-unidade

Support tickets require unidade field — comms may target wrong campus if guardian linked to
wrong matrícula.

## Live class links in Aulas

Depends on Google Meet integration; broken link surfaces in app Aulas filter views.

## Analytics not on comms engagement

Proesc Analytics covers finance/enrollment/grades/diary delivery — **not** comunicado read rates
in harvested articles.

## Proesc Sign overlap

Contract signature may occur in trilha web flow separate from Agenda chat — families confuse
channels.
