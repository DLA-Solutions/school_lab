# Glossary — Sophia terminology

| Our term | Sophia | Definition | Decision |
|----------|--------|------------|----------|
| guardian portal | Terminal Web / Portal do aluno | Web portal for guardians and students | `guardian_portal` |
| teacher workspace | Ferramenta Professor | Web module for grades, content, attendance, tasks, occurrences | `teacher_workspace` |
| occurrence | ocorrência | Pedagogical or disciplinary event on a student | `occurrence` |
| enrollment | matrícula | Student intake with contracts and documents | `enrollment` |
| re-enrollment | rematrícula | Returning-student contract flow with e-signature | `re_enrollment` |
| lead | prospectivo / Gestor de Vendas | CRM lead linked from RD Station | `lead` |
| receivable | conta a receber / título | Tuition and fee billing item | `receivable` |
| boleto | boleto bancário | Bank slip; batch generation supported | `boleto` |
| e-signature | Clicksign (integração) | Electronic contract signature | `document_acceptance` |
| NFS-e | nota fiscal de serviço | Service invoice for tuition | `service_invoice` |
| product NF | nota fiscal de produto | Product/material invoice | `product_invoice` |
| recurring card | pagamento recorrente com cartão | Monthly card charge without full-limit hold | `recurring_card_payment` |
| delinquency | inadimplência | Overdue receivables management | `delinquency` |
| WhatsApp channel | integração WhatsApp web | Outbound communication channel | channel adapter |
