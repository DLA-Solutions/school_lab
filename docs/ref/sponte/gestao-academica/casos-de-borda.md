# Edge cases — academic management (Sponte)

Harvest: 2026-08-14.

## Segment: language schools vs basic education

Several pedagogical features are explicitly **unavailable for language-school segment**:
online assessments, Agenda Plus app, document editor customization. Schools on wrong
segment cannot self-serve those features.
([gestão pedagógica](https://www.sponte.com.br/gestao-pedagogica))

## Guardian cannot see grades

FAQ lists “not seeing grades” as top family issue. Resolution path is always:
verify portal URL, school-issued login, then secretariat — not vendor self-service KB.
([suporte](https://www.sponte.com.br/suporte))

## Per-school portal URL

Each school has an **exclusive Portal do Aluno link**; families cannot guess URL.
Credential and link distribution is entirely school responsibility.
([suporte](https://www.sponte.com.br/suporte))

## Teacher credential lockout

Invalid teacher login for grade entry → secretariat reset only; Sponte support is
tertiary channel after school admin.
([suporte](https://www.sponte.com.br/suporte))

## Legacy mobile apps deprecated

FAQ states legacy store apps removed except “Portal de Notas”; substitute is Agenda Plus.
Schools mid-migration may have mixed family instructions.
([suporte](https://www.sponte.com.br/suporte))

## Document editor segment lock

Schools expecting custom boletim HTML editor may discover feature disabled for their
segment after sales — high migration surprise risk.
([gestão pedagógica](https://www.sponte.com.br/gestao-pedagogica))

## ERP sync downstream (ClassApp et al.)

Roster changes in Sponte must propagate to integrated comms products; year-start sync
is critical path for partner schools (documented in ClassApp corpus, not Sponte public pages).

## Evaluation model complexity

Marketing emphasizes “personalized” models with recoveries and BNCC skills — implies
many configuration dimensions before teachers can launch diaries; no public self-service
troubleshooting articles.
