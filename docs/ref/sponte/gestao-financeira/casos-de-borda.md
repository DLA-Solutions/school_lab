# Edge cases — financial management (Sponte)

Harvest: 2026-08-14.

## Mensalidade Garantida eligibility

Program marketed for **basic education** only; language schools and other segments may
not qualify — schools may assume guarantee applies universally.
([mensalidade garantida](https://www.sponte.com.br/mensalidade-garantida))

## Guaranteed tuition vs standard dunning

Schools in the program may still run régua for non-covered fees; public docs do not
clarify fee-type scope — risk of double pressure on families.

## Fiscal homologation dependency

Automated NFS-e requires municipal homologation; failure blocks issuance — no public
troubleshooting KB (support in-app only).

## WhatsApp payment links

Links sent via WhatsApp depend on guardian phone accuracy in secretariat cadastro;
wrong number = silent non-payment (inferred).

## “Zero reconciliation” claim

Marketing asserts no manual bank reconciliation; edge cases (partial payments, chargebacks)
not documented publicly — support channel required.

## Sponte Pay onboarding

Payment rail setup likely vendor-led (like Edukante pattern); no self-service checklist in
public corpus — delays first boleto.

## Family financial view in app

Guardians see financial module in Agenda Plus; desync between ERP permission and app
visibility not documented (login issues defer to secretariat).

## Delinquency AI alerts

Risk scoring mentioned in marketing without guardian-facing transparency — potential LGPD
question on automated profiling (open question).
