# Edge cases — communication (Sponte)

Harvest: 2026-08-14.

## No public help center for comms failures

Chat/notification issues route to in-app “Atendimento Online” or secretariat — no searchable
KB for “message not received”.
([suporte](https://www.sponte.com.br/suporte))

## Segment: Agenda Plus unavailable

Language-school segment may lack app — families forced to portal-only; marketing pages
do not surface this on comms landing page.
([gestão pedagógica](https://www.sponte.com.br/gestao-pedagogica))

## Per-school portal URL discovery

Families without correct URL cannot onboard; school must distribute link manually every year.

## Email verification on first login

Guardian changes email at school but app still has old email → verification codes fail until
secretariat updates cadastro.

## Dual platform schools

Schools using ClassApp + Sponte risk duplicate notifications if both push occurrences/payments
unless integration carefully scoped.

## WhatsApp as payment channel not message channel

WhatsApp emphasized for **payment links**, not two-way audited messaging — different from
Agenda Edu / ClassApp multicanal positioning.

## Legacy app confusion

FAQ references deprecated store apps; families may install wrong binary and see missing grades.

## Contract signature on wrong guardian device

E-signature tied to portal session; multi-guardian households not detailed in public Sponte pages.
