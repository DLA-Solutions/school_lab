# Flows — communication (Sponte)

Harvest: 2026-08-14.

## School announces event to families

**Actor:** secretariat / coordination  
**Preconditions:** event in school calendar

1. Register event in ERP calendar module.
2. Families receive notification via Agenda Plus (push).
3. Families view details in app calendar.

Source: [app agenda](https://www.sponte.com.br/app-de-agenda-escolar)

## Guardian first app access

**Actor:** guardian  
**Preconditions:** email registered by school

1. Download Sponte Agenda Plus.
2. Log in with school-registered email.
3. Set password; enter email verification code; accept terms.
4. Access home hub (grades, finance, chat, calendar).

Source: [suporte](https://www.sponte.com.br/suporte)

## Notify occurrence to family

**Actor:** teacher / secretariat  
**Preconditions:** occurrence recorded in ERP

1. Register school occurrence on student record.
2. System pushes notification to guardian app.

Source: [app agenda](https://www.sponte.com.br/app-de-agenda-escolar)

## Electronic contract signature

**Actor:** guardian  
**Preconditions:** contract generated at enrollment

1. Open contract in portal or app.
2. Complete electronic signature workflow.

Source: [funcionalidades](https://www.sponte.com.br/funcionalidades), [portal do aluno](https://www.sponte.com.br/portal-do-aluno)

## Send payment reminder

**Actor:** system / financial staff  
**Preconditions:** overdue or upcoming installment

1. Trigger régua or manual send.
2. Deliver payment link via SMS, email, or WhatsApp.

Source: [gestão financeira](https://www.sponte.com.br/gestao-financeira)
