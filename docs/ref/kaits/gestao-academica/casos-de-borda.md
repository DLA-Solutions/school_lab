# Edge cases — academic management (KAITS)

Harvest: 2026-08-14.

## Legal vs financial guardian split

Student registration distinguishes **legal** and **financial** guardians — billing
and authorization may route to different contacts.
→ Source: [escola infantil](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-escola-infantil/)

## Evaluation period flexibility

Schools may use bimestre, trimestre, or other periodization — system must not
assume a single national calendar.
→ Source: [educação básica](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-escola-educacao-basica/)

## Lesson cancellation without makeup

Institution defines whether cancelled lessons require replacement; rules are
per-school, not hard-coded.
→ Source: [homepage](https://kaits.com.br/)

## Multiple lesson formats in one institution

Same school may run turma, individual, VIP, and flexible classes — scheduling and
billing models differ per format.
→ Source: [cursos livres](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-cursos-livres/)

## Teacher hour calculation edge cases

Auto **hora/aula** calculation depends on scheduled vs delivered lessons;
cancellations and makeups likely affect totals (inferred — not documented step-by-step).
→ Source: [escola de idiomas](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-escola-de-idiomas/) *(inferred)*

## Corporate employee leaves company

Partner portal shows employees enrolled — offboarding when employment ends is not
documented publicly.
→ Source: [escola de idiomas](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-escola-de-idiomas/) *(gap)*

## Early childhood photo sharing

Marketing recommends activity photos to parents but cites privacy rules — consent
and retention not specified.
→ Source: [blog infantil](https://kaits.com.br/educacao-infantil/)

## Portal embed on external site

School may embed portal on own domain; developer instructions provided offline —
integration failure modes not public.
→ Source: [homepage FAQ](https://kaits.com.br/)

## Unlimited staff users

Pricing is per active-student band, not per seat — many concurrent staff logins
without shared credentials.
→ Source: [homepage FAQ](https://kaits.com.br/)

## Higher-ed online class integration

Portal integrates with external online-class platforms; which providers and sync
scope are unspecified.
→ Source: [ensino superior](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-ensino-superior/) *(gap)*

## Re-enrollment reporting vs self-service flow

Secretariat gets **rematriculados** report; dedicated online rematrícula flow is
not described on public pages (unlike some competitors).
→ Source: [homepage](https://kaits.com.br/)

## Multi-unit data isolation

Franchise dashboard aggregates unit data — per-unit guardian isolation and
cross-unit student transfer not documented.
→ Source: [redes e franquias](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-redes-e-franquias/) *(gap)*

## Document acceptance without enrollment

Aceite online applies to multiple document types — may include non-enrollment
documents (inferred).
→ Source: [homepage](https://kaits.com.br/) *(inferred)*

## Grade type mixing

School may use both notes and concepts in the same evaluation system configuration
(marketing allows both).
→ Source: [educação básica](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-escola-educacao-basica/)

## Library MEC compliance scope

MEC-compliant cataloging is claimed for higher ed — applicability to basic education
library module unclear.
