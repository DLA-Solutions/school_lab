# Flows — financial management (KAITS)

Harvest: 2026-08-14.

## 1. Configure billing model (language school)

**Actor:** finance manager  
**Preconditions:** Institution segment = idiomas or flexible courses

1. Select billing strategy: per lesson, per hour, prepaid/postpaid credits,
   monthly, or package.
2. Link model to course/class/enrollment type.
3. System automates calculation to avoid manual errors.

Source: [escola de idiomas](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-escola-de-idiomas/)

## 2. Schedule automatic boleto issuance

**Actor:** finance staff (one-time setup)  
**Preconditions:** Receba Fácil or bank integration active

1. Configure issuance schedule for upcoming charges.
2. System generates boletos automatically.
3. Sends due-date reminder emails to payers.

Source: [educação básica](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-escola-educacao-basica/)

## 3. Guardian pays via portal (boleto second copy)

**Actor:** financial guardian / student  
**Preconditions:** Portal access; open receivable

1. Open finance section in portal or App KAITS.
2. Request second boleto copy (`segunda via`).
3. Pay via available rail; system records settlement.

Source: [educação básica](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-escola-educacao-basica/), [FAQ](https://kaits.com.br/)

## 4. Pix / payment link collection

**Actor:** payer  
**Preconditions:** Receba Fácil KAITS enabled

1. Receive payment link or access Pix QR in portal.
2. Complete payment outside or inside portal.
3. Integration with banking network updates receivable status.

Source: [homepage](https://kaits.com.br/)

## 5. Online enrollment with payment

**Actor:** prospect  
**Preconditions:** Published classes; payment rails configured

1. Select turma and complete registration.
2. Pay as part of enrollment funnel (card, boleto, Pix per enabled rails).
3. Accept contact online; enrollment becomes active.

Source: [homepage](https://kaits.com.br/), [escola de idiomas](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-escola-de-idiomas/)

## 6. Cash flow and expense planning

**Actor:** school administrator  
**Preconditions:** Cost plan and cash centers configured

1. Register expenses with category (plano de custo) and cash center.
2. Review cash flow with monthly fixed costs and contingencies.
3. Use reports/graphs for investment decisions.

Source: [blog educação básica](https://kaits.com.br/educacao-basica/), [homepage](https://kaits.com.br/)

## 7. Delinquency management

**Actor:** finance staff  
**Preconditions:** Overdue receivables exist

1. Identify delinquent accounts via finance reports.
2. Apply personalized collection (email alerts already sent preventively).
3. Track recovery through finance module (specific protest/legal steps not public).

Source: [homepage](https://kaits.com.br/), [educação básica](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-escola-educacao-basica/)

## 8. Issue service or product NF

**Actor:** finance staff  
**Preconditions:** Municipal integration configured

1. Generate NF-serviço for tuition/services.
2. Generate NF-produto for inventory/material sales.
3. Submit via prefeitura integration.

Source: [homepage FAQ](https://kaits.com.br/)

## 9. Sell teaching material (inventory)

**Actor:** secretariat / store operator  
**Preconditions:** Stock items registered

1. Record stock in/out by responsible user.
2. Sell and deliver material to student.
3. Financial entry + optional product NF.

Source: [homepage](https://kaits.com.br/)

## 10. Commercial retention email batch

**Actor:** commercial staff  
**Preconditions:** CRM data populated

1. Filter recipients: expiring enrollments, birthdays, interested former students.
2. Send batch emails for retention or conversion.
3. Track leads via CRM follow-up module.

Source: [homepage](https://kaits.com.br/), [cursos livres](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-cursos-livres/)

## 11. Franchise financial oversight

**Actor:** franchisor  
**Preconditions:** Network module active

1. Access real-time dashboards across units.
2. Review financial/analytic KPIs for network health.
3. Inform expansion strategy.

Source: [redes e franquias](https://kaits.com.br/sistema-para-redes-e-franquias/)
