# Flows — financial management (Edukante)

Harvest: 2026-08-14.

## 1. Create receivables from enrollment

**Actor:** secretariat  
**Preconditions:** Enrollment with financial plan

1. On create/edit enrollment, system generates receivable schedule.
2. Optionally add ad-hoc receivables to enrollment.
3. Navigate enrollment ↔ receivables bidirectionally.

Source: [sistema de gestão escolar](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/sistema-de-gestao-escolar.aspx)

## 2. Generate and send boleto (single)

**Actor:** secretariat or guardian via portal  
**Preconditions:** Receivable open; bank account configured by support

1. One-click boleto from receivable (payer, amount, due date, discounts, fines).
2. Send PDF via email/WhatsApp or print.
3. On payment, status auto-updates (no return file).

Source: [recursos](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/software-gestao-academica-financeira-para-escolas-cursos-faculdades-recursos.aspx)

## 3. Bulk boleto generation

**Actor:** finance staff  
**Preconditions:** Filter criteria (month, year, class, enrollment)

1. Select receivables via filters.
2. Generate all boletos at once; optional mass email send.

Source: [recursos](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/software-gestao-academica-financeira-para-escolas-cursos-faculdades-recursos.aspx)

## 4. Pix payment (portal)

**Actor:** student/guardian  
**Preconditions:** Pix enabled for school

1. Open receivable in portal.
2. Display QR code or copy-paste code.
3. Real-time payment recognition and receivable settlement.

Source: [financeiro recursos](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/software-gestao-financeira-para-escolas-cursos-faculdades-recursos.aspx)

## 5. Card payment with token

**Actor:** student/guardian  
**Preconditions:** Card gateway enabled

1. Register card → security token stored.
2. Pay current or future installments; optional recurring charge.
3. Real-time approval updates receivable.

Source: [sistema de gestão escolar](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/sistema-de-gestao-escolar.aspx)

## 6. Payment link without login

**Actor:** finance staff → guardian  
**Preconditions:** Open receivable

1. Generate secure payment link (card or Pix).
2. Send via email/WhatsApp.
3. Guardian pays without portal credentials.

Source: [financeiro recursos](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/software-gestao-financeira-para-escolas-cursos-faculdades-recursos.aspx)

## 7. Online enrollment payment

**Actor:** prospect  
**Preconditions:** Published online enrollment

1. Complete cart checkout.
2. Pay card/boleto/debit/transfer.
3. Auto-create student, enrollment, all future receivables, cash-flow entries.

Source: [controle acadêmico](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/software-gestao-escolar-web-controle-academico.aspx)

## 8. Delinquency management

**Actor:** finance staff  
**Preconditions:** Overdue receivables

1. View delinquency dashboard (amounts, students).
2. Send collection letter (custom template) by email.
3. Optional: protest overdue boletos via integrated routine.
4. Amounts reflect fines/interest per rules.

Source: [financeiro recursos](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/software-gestao-financeira-para-escolas-cursos-faculdades-recursos.aspx)

## 9. Bulk financial actions

**Actor:** finance staff  
**Preconditions:** Multiple receivables selected

1. Filter receivables (date, status, method, class, student, etc.).
2. Mass settle, change status, delete erroneous lines, or batch-generate boletos/Pix.

Source: [financeiro recursos](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/software-gestao-financeira-para-escolas-cursos-faculdades-recursos.aspx)

## 10. Product/service sale

**Actor:** secretariat  
**Preconditions:** Catalog configured

1. Select products/services; identify payer (PF/PJ).
2. Generate receivable with boleto/Pix/card options.
3. Decrement stock for physical items; receipt on settlement.

Source: [financeiro recursos](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/software-gestao-financeira-para-escolas-cursos-faculdades-recursos.aspx)

## 11. Expense registration

**Actor:** finance staff  
**Preconditions:** Supplier/category tree configured

1. Register expense with classification.
2. Expense appears in cash flow and expense reports.

Source: [recursos](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/software-gestao-academica-financeira-para-escolas-cursos-faculdades-recursos.aspx)

## 12. Payroll payment

**Actor:** HR/finance  
**Preconditions:** Employees and contract types configured

1. Register payroll run per contract type (taxes applied).
2. Payments recorded as cash outflows.

Source: [recursos](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/software-gestao-academica-financeira-para-escolas-cursos-faculdades-recursos.aspx)

## 13. NFS-e generation

**Actor:** finance staff  
**Preconditions:** NFS-e accounts configured

1. Select receivables for service invoicing.
2. System calculates taxes; issues NFS-e; retains history.

Source: [financeiro recursos](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/software-gestao-financeira-para-escolas-cursos-faculdades-recursos.aspx)

## 14. Daily settlement reconciliation

**Actor:** finance manager  
**Preconditions:** Online payments enabled

1. Open bank transfer view for settlement date.
2. Expand batch to see receivables included; reconcile fees.

Source: [recursos](https://www.edukante.com/sistemas-gestao-escolar/software-gestao-academica-financeira-para-escolas-cursos-faculdades-recursos.aspx)
